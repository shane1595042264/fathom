You are blind in the deep. Your only sense is sonar — tap to ping, and the flooded dark
lights up for a heartbeat. But the thing down here is blind too, and it hunts the exact
sound you make to see. Descend forever. Ping less. Survive longer. Death is one touch.

## Long description

**One button to see. The same button is what kills you.**

The deep is black. Your lamp is dead. All you have left is a sonar **ping** — an
expanding ring of light that paints the flooded labyrinth for a moment, then fades,
leaving you to swim through the dark from memory.

But you are not alone, and the Angler is blind too. It hears **everything** — every
ping, every panicked stroke, every wall you blunder into, every gasp for air. Every
time you light the world to find your way, you tell it exactly where you are.

That is the whole game, and it never lets go of you: **light now means attention now.**

- 🔦 **Ping to see — or don't.** The single, perfect double-bind: the only way to
  navigate is the only way to get found. Every ping is a decision.
- 🫁 **Breathe or drown.** Oxygen drains constantly. Air pockets refill it, but
  surfacing is *loud*. And the closer the Angler gets, the faster you breathe — panic
  itself suffocates you.
- 🌊 **Descend forever.** A procedural flooded maze that grows denser, darker, and more
  lethal with every depth, while the hunters get faster and more numerous. Like Tetris
  speed — easy to learn, impossible to master.
- 🩸 **One touch and you're gone.** Score is how deep you got. See your epitaph. Smash
  Descend again.
- 🎧 **Audio is the gameplay.** Fully procedural, directional sound — you hear the
  creature before you ever see it. Best in the dark, with headphones.
- ♿ **Built to be played by everyone** — subtitles for audio cues, reduced flashing,
  colorblind-safe danger, screen-shake and control options, full controller support.

Minimalist. Merciless. The light you summon to survive is the light that finds you.

**How deep can you go before you have to look?**
